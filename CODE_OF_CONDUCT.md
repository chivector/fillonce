# Code of Conduct

We want FillOnce to be a welcoming project for people across languages, countries, professions, abilities, and levels of experience.

Be respectful, assume good intent while taking impact seriously, give actionable technical feedback, and never publish another person's private documents or personal data. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
